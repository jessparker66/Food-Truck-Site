# Food-Truck-Site
## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones: Menu cards are in 1 column, the Visit us boxes are stacked, and the heading is smaller.
- From md (768px): Cards go to 2 columns, the Visit us boxes sit side by side, the heading and intro text get bigger, and there's more spacing.
- From lg (992px): Cards go to 3 columns, and there's more space on the sides.
